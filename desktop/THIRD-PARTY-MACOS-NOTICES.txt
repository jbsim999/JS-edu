No Developer ID, notarization, downloaded-archive Gatekeeper approval or public Release
publication is performed by this packaging workflow. CI uploads only single-recipient
encrypted app archives and plaintext build/test evidence, not publicly usable ZIPs.

Before a public binary release, provide the exact corresponding source, build/patch
instructions and redistribution terms required by linked LGPL/MPL and other components.
The upstream links above are references, not an attached corresponding-source archive
or a public distribution license-compliance sign-off.
JavaScript-edu's own code/course licensing is separate; public source visibility does
not establish a redistribution license for application or course content.
